# ADR-0038: A friendlier design language

Status: Accepted (founder-decided 2026-09-26; supersedes parts of ADR-0023 and SPEC §11.1–§11.4)

**Amended by ADR-0042 (D-232):** "light first" no longer names the default mode: a new install opens in Aurora dark;
light stays designed and measured in every set that has one.

## Context

On 2026-09-26 the founder used the TestFlight and Play internal builds on his
own phones and reported that SKIPTI "looks like a technical software": the tab
labels, the monospace figures, the uppercase letter-spaced labels and the
grey-on-grey steel palette read as an engineering tool, "intimidating for
non-technical users". He asked for research into what makes an app intuitive
for everyone, a more vibrant and dynamic palette ("the colour palette is fine
but we need to make it more vibrant"), five tabs with a raised add button in
the middle, a Groups tab, and a Balance that shows every group.

SPEC §11 had derived the look from vardrlabs.com: "dark technical surface,
hairline rules as the primary structural device, uppercase letterspaced
micro-labels with middot separators, monospace with tabular figures for every
number". That is the register he is asking to leave.

A proposal was put to him as a canvas of eight screens
(`https://claude.ai/artifact/Ajd2wQY19v1RcKDDD6xjBi`, generated by
`.drafts/redesign/gen.py`), with the research behind it:

- **Familiarity (Jakob's law).** People arrive knowing Splitwise and Tricount:
  a headline balance, groups as cards, a prominent add, "settle up". Published
  redesign studies of both apps found users defeated by navigation and
  terminology, not by missing features.
- **Fewer choices at once (Hick's law, progressive disclosure).** Adding an
  expense shows the amount, what it was for and a category first; the group,
  payer and split start from sensible defaults, and the split types sit
  behind "Split".
- **Money between friends is awkward.** Research on bill splitting finds many
  people would rather overpay than ask. The copy is warm and names the next
  step ("You'll get back", "Rahul pays you", "Remind", "I paid Ana"); owing is
  a warm coral, never an alarm red; a settled group is celebrated.
- **Recognition over recall.** Every tab carries an icon and a label; each
  group has a colour tile and each person coloured initials.
- **The aesthetic-usability effect.** An interface people find pleasant is
  perceived as easier, and forgiven more.

Apple's Human Interface Guidelines say a tab bar should navigate rather than
perform an action. The raised centre add button is nonetheless a widely used,
accepted pattern (Instagram, Venmo); the founder asked for it and it is kept.

## Decision

The founder chose **the proposal's look**, **one balance total per currency**
(never converted), and the five tabs **Balance · Groups · (+) · Expenses ·
Settle up**.

1. **Colour: the same hues, turned up.** Token names are kept; values change
   in all three modes (light, dark, Night), and new tokens are added:

   Every text colour clears 4.5:1 on every surface in every mode (the lowest,
   owing on surface-raised in light, 4.55), and each money colour clears 4.5:1
   on its own soft fill (`.drafts/d174/tokens.py`). **The money pair stays
   distinguishable without colour vision:** the teal-leaning green was chosen
   over an emerald because, simulated (Machado 2009), its separation from the
   coral is ΔE 62.9 for deuteranopia and 47.2 for protanopia, against 37.8 and
   19.6 for ADR-0023's pair (`.drafts/redesign/cvd.py`). Words still carry the
   meaning ("you owe", "owes you"), so colour is never the only carrier.

2. **Group colours.** Six tones (coral, violet, teal, amber, sky, pink), each a
   soft fill with a saturated letter at 4.5:1 or better, chosen from the
   group's id so every device shows a group in the same colour.

3. **Type: one typeface.** Space Grotesk (bundled in both apps since 2026-08-24) for everything,
   with tabular figures for amounts. IBM Plex Mono stays only where letter
   shapes must be unambiguous to copy by hand: the Recovery Kit's words and the
   six-word device phrase. Section labels are sentence case, not uppercase and
   letter-spaced.

4. **Shapes.** Cards on the page with 20-point corners; buttons 52 points tall
   with 14-point corners. The primary action is filled with the accent; the
   secondary is tonal (accent-soft fill, accent label), which reads as a
   button without an outline.

5. **Structure** (their own units): five tabs with the raised add in the
   middle; a Groups tab that creates, joins and shows every group with its
   numbers; a Balance that covers every group, with one total per currency;
   a group page with its statistics; add as one sheet; Expenses across every
   group; Settle up in plain words.

## What this supersedes

- SPEC §11.1's direction (the technical register) and §11.3's rules that
  every number is monospace and that labels are uppercase micro-labels.
- ADR-0023's values (its principle stands: light first, dark derived, every
  pair measured, a colour-blind-safe money pair).
- SPEC §11.4's single balance line becomes the per-group and per-person
  breakdowns under Balance; the rule that a balance says who and how much, in
  one glance, stands.

What stays: light first, Night (ADR-0036), Liquid Glass only on iOS system
chrome, 44/48-point touch targets, Dynamic Type and font scaling to 200%, the
copy's honesty about security (§11.6), and GUARANTEES in full.

## Consequences

- Every screen changes appearance at once when the tokens change; the
  structural units follow.
- The contrast tests on both shells (AppearanceTests, AppearanceTest) are
  re-pinned to the new values and keep enforcing the floors.
- Screenshots in older BUILD-LOG entries show the previous look; they are a
  record, not a reference.

## Revision: Aurora and glass (D-176, the founder, 2026-09-26)

Seen on his phone, D-174's cobalt read as "kinda generic like every other
app". Offered four directions, he chose **Aurora**: a northern-lights palette,
a nod to SKIPTI's Old Norse name without being the later Norse theme.

- **Primary** violet-indigo `#6246EA` (dark and Night `#A99BFF`); grounds lean
  violet (light `#F5F4FB` mist, dark `#0C0B16` ink); every other token moves
  with them. The money pair is unchanged. Every floor above still holds; the
  lowest pair is owing on surface-raised in light, 4.53:1
  (`.drafts/d176/aurora.py`).
- **The glow**: three soft blooms (teal `#2DD4BF`, violet `#8B5CF6`, rose
  `#F472B6`) behind the screens, drifting slowly; decorative only, stopped by
  Reduce Motion (iOS) and "remove animations" (Android). The hero gradient
  `#3A2A9E → #5B3FD9 → #7A3AB8 → #A8327E` keeps white text at 6.1:1 or better.
- **Glass, wherever it adds depth** (he: "everywhere that seems aesthetic, it
  doesn't matter if it costs battery"). This widens ADR-0023's "Liquid Glass on
  iOS system chrome only": cards are frosted over the glow (iOS: the thin
  material with the surface at 78% over it; Android: the surface at 82%, no
  blur needed over the already-soft blooms), 24-point corners. Text keeps its
  measured contrast because it sits on the surface, not on the glow.
- The Danger zone's actions are tonal in the owe coral.

**Later, recorded for the founder (not built):** selectable themes: "Vardr
Labs" (ADR-0023's steel palette), a pure **Norse** theme (black, gold and
silver, runes and Nordic ornament), and Aurora as the default.
