# D-220 C2 "Shapes and chrome per theme" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Nothing round inside a square Vardr Labs card or a notched Norse card, the small shared controls (pills, bars,
disabled buttons, the currency list) drawn the same on both phones, and no purple.

**Architecture:** One decision per shell for each shape (`ThemeArt.pillKind`, `PillPaint.of`, `meterKind`,
`cardShape`, Vardr Labs' square panels, `TileClip.drawnSide`) and one constant per shared value (`pillChamfer`,
`pillOutline`, `ButtonLook.disabledAlpha`, the bar's blur film), each compared across shells by a parity test that reads
the Swift file. Then each shell's call sites move to them in two parallel lanes (iOS, Android) with disjoint files. The
bars get their own pair of tasks: iOS's Norse and Vardr Labs bars go solid, Android's Aurora bars blur what scrolls under
them (API 31+).

**Builds on D-219 (C1, `.drafts/d219/plan.md`), which merges first:** `LucideStroke` (2.4, every Lucide drawing here
uses it unchanged), `TabBarGround` (`glass` Aurora, `solid` Norse and Vardr Labs; iOS `filmAlpha` 0.72, Android
`FILM_ALPHA` 0.94), the 27-key `ThemeArt.metrics` table, `.drafts/d219/art.py`. C2 adds keys 28 and 29 and extends
`TabBarGround` on Android; it changes no C1 value.

## Design

1. **The pill (L19), one decision per shell, reused by E4.**
   - Shape: `ThemeArt.pillKind(theme)`: Aurora `.capsule`, Norse `.notched(pillChamfer)`, Vardr Labs `.square`;
     `ThemeArt.pillShape(theme)` draws it. **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): Norse pills are notched at 4**, by
     the founder's 2026-10-15 rule ("anything rectangular follows the notch", the audit's own call) and by the button's
     proportion (8 on a 52 pt button is 0.15; the pills here are 19 to 33 pt tall, so 4 is 0.12 to 0.21, art.py
     section 13). The Look Book's radius 2 (LB 240) is the pre-notch drawing.
   - Look: `PillLook.of(theme)` follows `ButtonLook`: `.filled` in Aurora and Norse, `.outlined` in Vardr Labs (LB 269:
     transparent, a 40% accent border, Plex Mono, which Vardr Labs' `label` and `microLabel` faces already are).
   - Paint: `PillPaint.of(tone, look, onPage)` is pure and returns token names and alphas (table in Global
     Constraints). Six tones: `accent`, `owner`, `selected`, `unselected`, `hero`, `photo`. Outlined keeps each tone's
     ink for the words and draws the border in it (at `pillOutline` 0.4, or 1 for `selected` and `unselected`, which
     are controls). `photo` stays filled in every theme (white words over a photo need their film).
   - **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): an outlined pill on the page ground fills with `bg`.** Accent words
     straight on the Vardr Labs light grid's darkest dot measure 4.12 (art.py 13a), so the filter chips and the currency
     chip (`onPage: true`) mask the dots under their words with the page colour, which looks transparent.
   - Twins: iOS `.themePill(_ tone:, onPage:)` (foreground, fill, border, shape; the call site keeps its font and
     padding, padding before the modifier); Android `Modifier.themePill(tone, dark, onPage)` (clip, fill, border) and
     `ThemePills.text(tone, dark)` for the words' colour.
   - E4's progress pill is `.themePill(.accent)` plus its Vardr Labs dot; E4 adds nothing to the shape.
2. **Panels square in Vardr Labs (L20).** `panelShape` / `panelKind` take `min(radius, theme.cardRadius)` outside Norse:
   Aurora unchanged (every call site asks 10 to 18, under 20), Vardr Labs 0. It reaches every panel (stat tiles, About's
   cards, expense detail, Settle, Group settings, removal panels). **DECIDED (controller, for the founder)** because the
   Look Book squares everything in a Vardr Labs card (LB 267) and `buttonRadius` already follows the same `min`.
3. **The card's shape as one decision; photo frames (L21).** `ThemeArt.cardShape(theme, radius:)`: `CardStyle.glass`
   a continuous rounded rectangle at `GlassCard.resolvedRadius` (Aurora keeps an asked radius), `.notched`
   `NotchedRectangle(chamfer 8)`, `.framed` `Rectangle()`. `photoFrame(radius:)` clips a photo to it and draws the
   theme's edge: Norse a 1 pt `norseEdge` stroke on the notch, Vardr Labs `FrameMarks` (inset 3, length 8, 1 pt) in
   **white at `frameAlpha` (DECIDED, controller, for the founder: the photo's own words are white; `textDim` marks vanish
   on a dark photo)**, Aurora none. The Groups photo card uses `radius: nil`, the group page's photo band `radius: 18`.
   The band's round add-people button takes `.themePill(.photo)` (a circle in Aurora as today, notched, square).
4. **Meters and swatches (L22).** `ThemeArt.meterKind(theme)`: Aurora `.capsule`, Norse and Vardr Labs `.square`
   (LB 241, 280); `meterShape` draws it (Android's capsule is `RoundedCornerShape(50)`, so iOS's `Capsule` and Android's
   5 dp on 10 dp, already equal, become one decision). The legend swatch: `swatchShape(theme)`, radius 2 in Aurora,
   square elsewhere. **E4's settled meter reuses `meterKind`.**
5. **Norse diamonds at full size (L23).** `TileClip.drawnSide(shape, size:)`: `size * sqrt(2)` for `.diamond`, `size`
   otherwise. The tile keeps a `size` footprint; the diamond is drawn centred on it at the drawn side and overflows by
   0.207 x size each side (art.py section 15: clear of the neighbour at every call site, 6.21 to 11.60 against 10 to 14

   square is now 0.707 of the size, as large as the other shapes' letter needs); the 0.36 and 0.4 diamond branches go.
6. **Vardr Labs' hero Settle up (L24).** In `ButtonLook.outlined` the hero button is transparent with a 1 pt `onHero`
   border, the words `onHero`, uppercase at .08 em (`ButtonLook.labelCase`, `tracking`), square; pressed shows an
   `onHero` film at `heroChipOpacity` (art.py section 14: words 13.89 / 16.47, film 11.03 / 12.42). Aurora and Norse

7. **Gold ᛫ on body-drawn detail lines (L25).** `GroupsCopy.detailParts` and `OverviewCopy.settlesInParts` return the
   parts; the Groups plain card and the group page's tile row draw them with `Separator.text(parts, size:

8. **Blue replaces violet (L29).** `.drafts/d220/palette.py` (d212's generator with violet replaced): blue at violet's
   place in the six, so every group that was violet turns blue and no other group changes tone. OKLCH hue 264 in Aurora
   light (sky 237, the nearest tone, 27 degrees away; purple starts near 285); every letter 4.5:1 on its fill, Vardr
   Labs light nudged by the generator. **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): blue**, because the hue wheel between
   amber (73) and teal (180) holds the money greens (owing 142, accent 170), and blue sits in the gap violet leaves
   between sky and pink. The case is renamed `blue` / `BLUE`.
9. **Bars (L-D217-3).** One ground decision for both bars on both shells: C1's `TabBarGround.of(theme)`.
   - iOS navigation bars: `BarGround.standard(theme)` and `.scrollEdge(theme)` built fresh per theme: Norse and Vardr
     Labs an opaque `surface` with a `hairline` shadow line (`configureWithOpaqueBackground`), Aurora the system default
     (`configureWithDefaultBackground`: the system's glass and scroll-edge effect); the scroll edge transparent in every
     theme (the bar reads clear at the top, as today). Set on the proxy and on every live bar in `TitleStyle.apply`,
     BEFORE `BarTint.applyBackButton` copies them (on iOS 17 to 25 its proxy path starts from `BarGround.standard`, not
     a bare `UINavigationBarAppearance()`).
   - Android, Norse and Vardr Labs: the bar's ground is `surface` at full alpha (times the fade-in), not 0.94.
   - Android, Aurora: `Backdrop` (new file `Backdrop.kt`, own code, no dependency): the page's scrolling content is
     recorded into a `GraphicsLayer` (`rememberGraphicsLayer()`, Compose UI 1.12), drawn as usual except under the bar
     while the bar's ground is on, and the bar draws that layer again, offset to its own rect, through a second layer
     with `BlurEffect(BLUR_RADIUS, BLUR_RADIUS)`, then the `surface` film at `BLUR_FILM_ALPHA` 0.72 (iOS's film).
     Below API 31 (no `RenderEffect`): the content is still clipped out from under the bar and the film is 0.94 (C1's).
     Wired in `TabRootScaffold`, `PushedPage`, `SheetPage` and the shell's tab bar (`HomeShell` provides one backdrop
     through `LocalShellBackdrop`; the tab root's scaffold records into it; a source that leaves composition clears it,
     so the tab bar never draws a stale page). A `PushedBar` used on its own (lazy lists, the currency list) takes an
     optional `backdrop`; any left unwired keeps the film and is listed in the report.
     **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): blur radius 20 dp, film 0.72**, judged side by side with iOS at the look.
   - Android's `BarCircle` and `BarPill` get a 1 dp `hairline` ring (iOS 26's glass controls carry a rim; on a scrolled
     surface bar Android's white circle vanished, al-13).
10. **Disabled (L-D217-4).** `ButtonLook.disabledAlpha` 0.45 (iOS's existing value, now named). Android draws the
    ENABLED colours and multiplies by it (`Modifier.alpha`), for Primary (filled and outlined), Secondary and Danger
    (tonal and Vardr Labs' text look); no more `surfaceRaised` / `textFaint` disabled colours. Android's `FormActionBar`
    backs its actions with `bg` in the control shape, as iOS's `FormActionBar` does, so rows never show through a
    dimmed button. Profile's "Save name" takes the full width (iOS's `SecondaryButtonStyle` is full width).
11. **The currency list (L-D217-6).** iOS unchanged in structure; its two SF Symbols become Lucide `search` and
    `check` (copied verbatim from upstream, NOTICE and the licence inventory in the same commit). Android keeps a
    `Dialog` window (the list opens from several sheets) but draws it as a pushed page: `decorFitsSystemWindows =
    false`, the window's dim amount 0, the art under the system bars, entering from the end in 250 ms (iOS's push; none
    under Reduce Motion), the `PushedBar` with a backdrop; the search field in its own card row with the Lucide
    `search` glyph; "Common" and "Everything" as section labels over `glassCard` cards of rows with hairline dividers;
    the code in `Type.numeric(17, FontWeight.Normal)` (iOS `numeric(17)` at 400), the name at `TypeScale.detail` in
    `textDim`, the selected row's Lucide `check` in the accent.
12. **Small fixes.** L28: Android's "People in X" on a plain Groups card is a plain `Text` with `clickable(role =
