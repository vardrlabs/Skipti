# D-223 Home "Your ledgers and Lately" Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Home becomes the Look Book's Home on both phones: today's hero plus the who-owes line, "Your ledgers" as a
two-column grid of ledger cards, each with its settled meter, a "Lately" card from the activity feed, and the People list
below them. The same unit also twins the Groups cards' height and the Expenses empty state.

**Architecture:** Values, copy and pure decisions come first on both shells. That means 9 art values, 3 type sizes, the
`inbox` Lucide drawing, `WhoOwes`, `RelativeWhen`, `LedgerCards`, `LedgerGrid` and `Lately`. Each is compared across
shells, either by a parity test that reads the Swift file or by one golden table pinned in both tests. Then come each
shell's views, in two parallel lanes with disjoint files. Money strings come from core (`ffiMoneyFormat`, through the
existing `money(...)` helpers). Each ledger card's meter is E4's `SettledMeter`, small size, fed by E4's
`ffi_settled_progress`. Lately reads D-189's `ActivityFeed` and needs no new state.

**Tech Stack:** SwiftUI (iOS 17+), Jetpack Compose, XCTest, JUnit. No core change.

## Design

1. **Home's order (Q5 a, LB JS 361-368).** Both shells, top to bottom:
   1. The Norse ornament (as today) and `HomeGreeting`.
   2. C2's `UsernameCard` when shown.
   3. The hero.
   4. "Your ledgers" (`.sectionLabel()` / `SectionLabel`, as "People" draws today) and the grid, when there are groups.
   5. The Lately card, when it has entries.
   6. "People" and its card (unchanged), when there are people.
   7. The error line.

   "By group" and its horizontal scroller go (`OverviewCopy.byGroup` / `BY_GROUP` removed). L55's halo goes with the
   scroller.
2. **The hero's who-owes line (Q4 a, LB 93).** It sits under the amount, inside the amount's stack, at
   `TypeScale.heroLine` (15) in `onHero` at 0.82 (the hero's dim, already used by "You'll get back"). It is joined by
   `Separator.text(parts, size: TypeScale.heroLine)` / `Separator.annotated(parts)`, so Norse gets the gold ᛫ (art.py 33:
   8.09). It is at most two lines. `WhoOwes.parts(people, currency, format)` takes the first two people of
   `overview.people` whose currency is the hero's (`totals.first`), in core's order (largest first, D-180).
   - A positive net reads "\(name) owes you \(amount)".
   - A negative net reads "You owe \(name) \(amount)" when it comes first, else "you owe \(name) \(amount)".
   - Amounts come from `ffiMoneyFormat` of the magnitude.
   - With no people in that currency, or in the settled hero, the line is not drawn.

   The chips ("You get back ..." per other currency, "N groups") and Settle up stay exactly as today and as C2 left them.
   **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): two people, largest first, in the hero's currency**, because SPEC §11.4 asks
   the balance to decompose into "who and how much ... sorted by magnitude". The People list right below names everyone.
3. **The ledger grid (L52).**
   - **Columns:** `LedgerGrid.columns(accessibilitySize:)` gives 2, or 1 at accessibility text sizes. That is iOS
     `dynamicTypeSize.isAccessibilitySize` and Android `fontScale >= 1.15f` (the app's existing switch, BalanceScreen.kt
     and SettleScreen.kt).
   - **Rows:** `LedgerGrid.rows(count:columns:)` chunks the cards in order. An odd last card takes one column, not the
     full width.
   - **Spacing:** `ledgerGridGap` (10) between columns and between rows.
   - **Row height:** cards in a row share it. iOS uses `Grid` / `GridRow` with each card at
     `.frame(maxHeight: .infinity, alignment: .topLeading)`. Android uses `Row(Modifier.height(IntrinsicSize.Min))` with
     each card at `weight(1f).fillMaxHeight()`.
4. **The ledger card (L54).**
   - **Card:** `glassCard()` at its default padding 16 on both shells (the LB's 12 x 1.28). The whole card opens the
     group, as today: iOS `NavigationLink { GroupPageScreen(groupID:) }` with `.buttonStyle(.plain)`; Android `.clip(ThemeArt.cardShape(...))`
     then `.clickable(onClickLabel = GroupsCopy.OPEN_HINT)`.
   - **Inside, spaced `ledgerCardGap` (6):**
     - A row: `GroupTile(group, size: ledgerTile 33)`, gap `ledgerTileGap` (9), then the name in
       `TypeRole.name(TypeScale.ledgerName)` (16), one line with an ellipsis.
     - The line, at `TypeScale.detail` (14), at most two lines.
     - E4's `SettledMeter` at `.small`.
   - **The line, from `LedgerCards.line(nets)`:**
     - With a first net > 0 it is `.owed`: "Owed " in `textDim`, then the amount in `TypeRole.numeric(TypeScale.detail, weight: 600)` in `owing`.
     - With a first net < 0 it is `.youOwe`: "You owe ", then the amount in `owed`.
     - With no net it is `.settled`: "Settled" (`OverviewCopy.settledShort`) in `textDim`.

     The amount is `money(|net|, code)` (core). iOS concatenates `Text`s; Android uses one `AnnotatedString` with
     `SpanStyle`s. Colours keep today's meaning (D-180): `owing` is money coming to you.
   - **The meter:** `LedgerCards.basisPoints(blocks, currency:)` reads E4's per-currency blocks for that group. It takes
     the block in the line's currency, else the first block (the settlement currency's, E4 Design 2). With no blocks it
     returns nil and no meter is drawn (a group with no expense or settlement). The fraction drawn is `bps / 10000`.
     **DECIDED (controller, for the founder)**, because a meter on a new group would read "0% settled" for a debt that
     never existed.
   - **Accessibility:** one element, "Goa trip, Owed ₹1,800.00, 72% settled" (`LedgerCopy.settledPercent(n)` =
     "\(n)% settled", n = `bps / 100` floored), with the hint `GroupsCopy.openHint`.
   - **Data:** Home's one read grows. `HomeOverview.load` also returns `progress: [groupId: [SettledProgressOut]]`,
     calling E4's `ffiSettledProgress` per group with the expenses, settlements and redistributions it already loaded
     (one vault read, as today). Android's `HomeOverview.Home` gains the same map.
5. **The Lately card (L53, Q2 a).**
   - **Card:** `glassCard()`. The first child is the label "Lately" (`.sectionLabel()` / `SectionLabel`, `text` colour,
     inside the card, as LB 368). Under it come the rows, spaced `latelyRowGap` (11, so stacked Norse diamonds clear,
     art.py 32), with no dividers.
   - **Entries:** `Lately.pick(entries)` takes the first `Lately.count` (3) of `ActivityFeed.entries(items, moved)`,
     newest first. That covers expenses others added, changed or deleted, and balances moved off a removed person,
     exactly what Recent activity lists.
   - **Hidden when empty:** no empty card on Home.
   - **Read only:** Home does not mark the feed seen.
   - **Row:**
     - `PersonAvatar(name:, size: latelyAvatar 33, fill: .soft)`, then gap `latelyAvatarGap` (12), then a stack of two lines.
     - The avatar uses the person shape per theme (K09 / Q3 a: Aurora circle, Norse diamond with its edge, Vardr Labs
       square), filled `accentSoft` with the initial in `accent`, display 700, through `TileClip.initialFont`.
     - First line: `ActivityFeed.line(kind, who, title)` in `TypeRole.name(TypeScale.feedLine)` (15), `textDim` for a
       deletion. A moved entry shows its `movedLine`.
     - Second line: `Separator.text([group name, amount, when], size: TypeScale.detail)` in `textDim`. A moved entry
       has no amount.
     - `who`: the member's name in that group, else `ActivityFeed.someone`. For a moved entry it is the history name of
       `from`, else `LedgerStore.unknownName`.
     - A row whose expense is not deleted and whose group is known opens the expense: iOS `NavigationLink { ExpenseDetailView(store:expenseID:named:) }`,
       Android `onOpenExpense(id)`, which goes to the existing `route = "expense/$id"` in AppRoot.
   - **Shared read:** each shell factors the screen's read into one shared function,
     `ActivityFeed.load(store, group:) -> ActivityFeed.Loaded(items, moved, movedLines, groups)`. Today's
     `ActivityScreen.load()` (iOS) and its `LaunchedEffect` (Android) become calls to it. ActivityScreen keeps its
     "seen" write, for group == nil only. iOS lifts the screen's private `Entry` to `ActivityFeed.Entry` with
     `ActivityFeed.entries(_:_:)`, the twin of Android's.
6. **When (both shells, one rule).** `RelativeWhen.of(atMs:nowMs:)` returns an enum:
   - `now` under 60 s (a negative delta reads as now);
   - `minutes(n)` under 60 min;
   - `hours(n)` under 24 h;
   - `days(n)` under 7 days;
   - `date` otherwise.

   The words are "now", "\(n) min", "\(n) h", "1 day", "\(n) days". `date` is the day and month from the CLDR skeleton
   "dMMM" in the phone's locale: iOS `DateFormatter.dateFormat(fromTemplate: "dMMM", options: 0, locale: .current)`,
   Android `android.text.format.DateFormat.getBestDateTimePattern(Locale.getDefault(), "dMMM")`. Lately and Recent
   activity's rows both use it; today's `.formatted(.relative(...))` and `DateUtils.getRelativeTimeSpanString` go.
   **DECIDED (controller, for the founder)** because the two system formatters word the same moment differently. The
   Look Book prints "2 min".
7. **The empty state (L-D217-10).** One `EmptyState(title:detail:)` view per shell, in the new HomeParts files:
   - Lucide `inbox` at `emptyIcon` (44) in `textDim`, stroked with `LucideStroke`.
   - 12 under the icon: the title in `TypeRole.name(TypeScale.emptyTitle)` (20) in `text`.
   - 4 under the title: the detail in `TypeRole.body()` (16) in `textDim`.
   - Centred, multiline-centred, padding 16, centred in the space under the bar.

   Expenses' empty state uses it with today's words ("Nothing here yet" / "Add your first expense.") on both shells.
   Android's one line splits into title and detail. Expenses' load error uses it too ("Could not load the ledger" / the
   error), replacing iOS's `ContentUnavailableView` and Android's two plain lines. The Norse ornament above it stays,
   with `TitleOrnament(above: 0)` unchanged. Balance's own empty title moves to `TypeRole.name(TypeScale.emptyTitle)` /
   `Type.name(dark, TypeScale.EMPTY_TITLE)`, so the two empty titles match. **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): a
   drawn Lucide `inbox` on both**, rather than dropping iOS's icon. It keeps what the founder has seen on iPhone without
   the system font, and the Look Book has no empty state to follow. NOTICE goes up by one drawing.
8. **Groups cards (L-D217-1).** The plain card's stack spacing comes from `ThemeArt.m("groupsCardSpacing")` (12, iOS's
   value today) on both shells. Android's `Arrangement.spacedBy(8.dp)` and the row's `heightIn(min = 48.dp)` go (the
   tile is 48 already). The look measures the card heights; the target is equal within 1 pt/dp.
9. **Room for "Just me" later (nothing built).** The grid takes an ordered list of cards, and the card takes its tile
   as a slot (`LedgerCard(name:line:basisPoints:) { tile }` / `LedgerCard(name, line, bps, dark, onClick) { tile() }`).
   `LedgerGrid.rows` lays out any count. Unit H (0.5.1) prepends one card with its own tile (ᛁ), name and line before the
   groups' cards. No grid, card or layout code changes. Nothing for it exists in this unit: no case, no flag, no copy.
