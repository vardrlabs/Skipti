## Design

1. **The art scale (Q8 a, L37, K10).** Every art length D-213 copied from the Look Book's px is multiplied by 1.28 (LB
   body 12.5 to 16, the small-type scale D-221 to D-225 used) and rounded to the whole point; the grid dot radius to 0.01.
   The diamond +'s radius follows C1's ratio (64 / 48), the same whole point. Two rules follow the chamfer: C2's pill
   notch is the button's chamfer at half the height (5), and the panel rule keeps its reason (the cut never more than a
   fifth of the panel) with both numbers scaled: 10 above 50 pt, else 8. The ornament's row grows to hold the 17 pt rune
   (1.15 em line box, rounded up to even: 20; the rule gives today's 16 for 13); the gap under the title stays 8. The grid's
   mean ink is unchanged (0.996 of today) because pitch and dot scale together. **DECIDED (controller, for the founder):
   the Norse hero's inner frame (5 to 6), ᚠ watermark (92 to 118, right 10 to 13, top -6 to -8) scale too**, the same
   1:1 copies by the same rule; they become metrics keys so both phones read one table. Shown to him in the before/after
   pairs; his pre-0.5.0 emulator look (on the release-gate list) is the mock-up Q8 a asked for.
2. **Stars over the curtains (Q7 a, L33).** The curtain layer draws first, then the eight stars source-over, on hero and
   page, both shells. Strengths unchanged (page 0.16, hero 1.0). Measured under D-214's recorded convention (motion.py
   part 1b, `each_alone`): curtains alone 4.61, a star alone 4.54, both hold. **Finding for the founder:** a star ON the
   curtain's brightest stop (the stacked pixel) reads textDim 2.98 with Q7 a, and 3.06 in today's order: today's page
   already fails a stacked reading, A does not open the gap; a page star strength that holds stacked is 0.006
   (invisible). Built as he chose; the stacked reading goes to him as a question (planner report).
3. **Norse page glows (L34).** The Look Book's `radial-gradient(90% 60% at 50% 0%, rgba(205,167,80,.08), transparent
   70%)` and `radial-gradient(120% 80% at 50% 120%, rgba(142,147,155,.07), transparent 60%)`, drawn under the runes with
   Dawn's technique (the context scaled to the ellipse's radii, a unit radial gradient clear at the stop). Colours reuse
   `ArtDraw.runeGoldHex` and `ThemeArt` `norseSilver`. Strength `norsePageGlow` 1.0. A rune (0.79) over the gold peak
   leaves textFaint at 3.91 (runes alone sit at exactly 4.50), and keeping textFaint would cap the glow at 0.
   **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): Norse's `groundFaint` takes `textDim` (#A69E89)**, D-214's own rule for Aurora
   (faint words on the page ground take the label colour); every other floor holds at the Look Book's strength (text
   11.38, textDim 5.31, accent 6.23). The 14 `Tokens.groundFaint` sites are unchanged; their Norse colour brightens.
4. **Norse hero ground (L31).** Norse's hero draws two stops (`heroStart`, `heroEnd`) at CSS 160 degrees for its own
   size: `CssGradient.unitPoints(degrees:width:height:)` (direction (sin, -cos), line length |w sin| + |h cos|, centred),
   pure and golden-tested (362 x 200: start (0.3527, -0.2324), end (0.6473, 1.2324); the function reproduces C1's bolt
   points for 150 degrees on 72 x 64). Aurora and Vardr Labs keep `Tokens.heroGradient` / `heroBrush`. The `heroMid`
   token stays (other sets use it; Norse's middle pixel is the same #12100A).
5. **The Norse notch edge (Q18 a, L35).** `ArtGeometry.notchEdges(rect, chamfer, inset 0.5)`: four segments on the
   straight sides, each ending where the 1 pt line's centre meets the diagonal, drawn clipped to the notched fill, so the
   corner cut is the Look Book's `clip-path` exactly. Used for the `norseEdge` stroke of every notched CARD surface: the
   `glassCard` `.notched` case and C2's `photoFrame` (both shells). **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): controls keep
   their whole outline** (buttons, inputs, pills, S2's switch), because Q18 named the card and an outline-only control
   with open corners reads broken; the Look Book clips only `.card`.
6. **Aurora glass twinned (L36).** iOS is the reference: `.ultraThinMaterial`, `surface` at 0.78, a 1 pt white 0.35 rim
   blended overlay, shadow black 0.06 radius 12 y 4 in both modes. Its literals move into `ThemeArt.metrics`
   (`glassFilm`, `glassRim`, `glassShadow*`) with no pixel change. Android:
   - film `glassFilm` 0.78 over the page art blurred at C2's `TabBarGround.BLUR_RADIUS` 20 dp on API 31+, through one
     app-wide art backdrop; `glassFilmNoBlur` 0.82 (today's measured value) on API 26 to 30 or when no art is recorded.
     Card words hold on both films over every page pixel (art.py 51; card_grounds already measured 0.78 and 0.82).
   - rim: the normal-blend white that reproduces iOS's overlay on the card: light `glassRimLight` 0.35 (exact: every
     channel of the light card is over 0.5, where overlay with white is white), dark `glassRimDark` 0.03 (within 4
     8-bit steps; today 0.08 is 19 steps over).
   - shadow: `Modifier.dropShadow` (Compose UI 1.12) black at `glassShadowAlpha` 0.06, offset y `glassShadowY` 4, in both
     modes (today a 6 dp elevation in light only), radius `ShadowBlur.radiusDp(glassShadowSigma 12, density)` =
     (sigma x density - 0.5) / 0.57735 / density (Compose's radius goes to Skia's BlurMaskFilter, sigma = 0.57735 r + 0.5
     px; iOS's shadow radius is taken as sigma, the CSS blur / 2): 20.45 dp at 2.625, 20.50 at 3.
   - the blur: an app-wide `ArtBackdrop` (C2's `Backdrop` class, a second instance) provided by `HomeShell` through
     `LocalArtBackdrop`, mirroring C2's `LocalShellBackdrop` ownership rule: the topmost attached `pageArt` in Aurora
     records its whole art (sky, curtains, stars) with its window origin; a page that leaves composition hands ownership
     back. Every page's art is the same pure function of time in window coordinates, so a card samples at its own window
     offset. `glassCard` in Aurora on API 31+ draws, under its film, the backdrop's layer translated by (art origin minus
     card origin), through its own layer with `BlurEffect(20 dp, 20 dp, TileMode.Clamp)`, clipped to the card's shape
     (C2's `backdropGlass` extended with a `shape` and `film` parameter, not copied). No recording below API 31.
     **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): blur 20 dp (C2's bar value) and film 0.78 (iOS's)**, judged side by side at
     the look; a card pixel more than 3 steps from iOS's at the same art point is reported with the film that would match
     (`a = (card - ground) / (surface - ground)` per channel, added to art.py by the look agent).
