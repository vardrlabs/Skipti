# 0.5.1 plan (planning only, nothing built)

**Revision 2, 2026-10-19 (D-236):** adds what D-234 and D-235 moved to 0.5.1: U17 the founder's profile name and
picture confirmation (split out of U9 so it can ship without the onboarding brainstorm), U18 zeroing the vault key's
copies (decision 123, with a correction: `zeroize` is NOT a new dependency), U19 clearing a group's parked entries when
membership returns, U20 group operations with `seen`, U21 and U22 the small shell and core/cloud leftovers, U23 the
Fable audit's findings. D-234's four (L2, L12 O2, L12, L13) were already U5 to U8. The scope flag and the order are
updated.

Drafted 2026-10-19 from WORKLOG (session 6 "0.5.1 ideas", session 7 RESUME "0.5.1 planning only", the founder's
2026-10-19 onboarding item), DECISIONS-TAKEN (36 x 37, 64, 66, 79), CLOUD-SECURITY line 553, the D-234 spec's
"Proposed for 0.5.1", BUILD-LOG section 4 (Just me, onboarding deferrals), and SPEC (5.8 settle-up hand-off, 10.2 App
Intents). D-numbers are given when each unit starts; the order below uses U-numbers.

Legend. Size: S (a day or less with gate and look), M (two to three days), L (a week or more). **FOUNDER** marks an item
that needs his own call before design (a guarantee, money, legal, anything public); everything else Opus decides on
evidence and records in DECISIONS-TAKEN. "G1" to "G7" are the seven guarantees (GUARANTEES.md).

**Scope flag (FOUNDER, first):** this list is two or three releases of work, not a patch. Recommended split (revision
2): **0.5.1** = security and correctness first: U0 to U4, U17 to U20, U22 and whatever U23 brings (about three weeks,
most of it gated on the Fable report); **0.5.2** = the twin design units 0.5.0 knowingly ships, U5 to U8 and U21 (about
three weeks; CLAUDE.md 10 calls each a defect, so they may move into 0.5.1 if the Fable report is light); **0.6.0** =
U9 to U16 (onboarding, Just me, Norse motion, Advanced mode, operator push and survey, payment hand-off, voice). The
version names and what ships when are his.
Also: if 0.5.0's Android upload has to be redone, Android becomes 0.5.1 / 5001 (`versions.md`), and this plan's
release takes the next free name.

### U18. Zero the vault key's copies (decision 123; core L4)
- **What:** `Zeroizing` wrappers for the key in `Skrin::open` (the `[u8; 32]` and the random `Vec`) and in
  `KeystoreBridge` (the `key.to_vec()` copy and the loaded `Vec`), so no copy outlives its use; a test that the
  wrappers are in place (source guard where behaviour cannot be observed) and sabotage rows. SQLCipher keeps the key
  while the vault is open regardless (THREAT_MODEL: a compromised phone is out of scope).
- **Correction to decision 123's premise (found in D-236):** the record says L4 "needs the `zeroize` crate, a new
  dependency". It does not: `core/Cargo.toml` already depends on `zeroize = "1"` (1.9.0 in `Cargo.lock`), and
  `skrin/mod.rs` already uses it for `MemoryKeystore`. `Zeroizing<T>` is in the crate without any extra feature, so the
  fix changes no dependency and needs no CONTRIBUTING rule 4 write-up. The founder chose 0.5.1 on that premise; tell
  him, and record it as "(Corrected D-NNN: ...)" in DECISIONS-TAKEN 123 when this unit starts. The shells' own copies
  (Swift `Data`, Kotlin `ByteArray` in the keystore bridges) are zeroed where the platform allows; what cannot be is
  recorded as residue.
- **Touches:** `core/src/skrin/`, the FFI keystore trait (no signature change expected; if one changes, `make
  bindings` and the core rebuilds, CLAUDE.md 8.5).
- **Size:** S. **FOUNDER:** nothing new (unless the shells need a new dependency, which they should not).
