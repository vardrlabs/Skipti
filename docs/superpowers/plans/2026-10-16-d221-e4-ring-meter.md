**Goal:** The four gamified touches the founder approved on 2026-10-13 (completion ring, settled meter, settled
celebration, group milestones), drawn as the Look Book draws them in every theme, the same on both phones, plus the two
cards the Look Book adds (the "next" card on You and on a group) and the group page's bare header.

every value lives in one table per shell compared across shells by a parity test that reads the Swift file. Views come
last, per shell, in parallel lanes with disjoint files. The celebration is drawn by D-214's engine (`ArtCanvas` /
`artCanvas`), a pure function of the time since it started, with no particles in still mode.

**Tech Stack:** Rust (skipti-core, UniFFI), SwiftUI (iOS 17+), Jetpack Compose, XCTest, JUnit.

## Design

1. **Settled progress lives in core.** `ffi_settled_progress(expenses, settlements, redistributions, group) ->
   Vec<SettledProgressOut>`: per currency of the group (sorted by code; a currency appears when the group has an
   expense or a settlement in it), `settled_minor` = the sum of the group's settlement amounts (a redistribution moves
   balances but is not a payment), `outstanding_minor` = the sum of every member's positive net (the same ledger
   `balance_line` builds, redistributions applied), `basis_points` = floor(settled x 10000 / (settled + outstanding)),
   10000 when both are 0, `settled` = outstanding == 0. Integer only (ADR-0002); the shells never add money. A group is
   "all settled" when the list is non-empty and every entry is settled. Goldens: `settled.out` (cases A to H and the
   negative). **DECIDED (controller, for the founder): the meter measures money moved over money that had to move**
   ("72% settled" = 72% of what was owed has been marked paid), because it rises with every payment and reaches 100
   exactly when nobody owes anybody; a debt-free group (everyone paid their own share) reads 100%.
2. **Multi-currency.** One block per currency inside the one Settled card, the group's settlement currency first, then
   by code; with more than one block each block's percent row names its currency in the kicker face. No FX is applied
   (amounts in different currencies are never added). **DECIDED (controller, for the founder)** because summing
   needs a rate the group may not have, and most groups have one currency.
3. **The setup steps (the spec's completion ring, GUARANTEES #5).** Without an account five steps, in this order: your
   name, a photo or emoji, a default currency, app lock, a first group. Signed in adds two: a username,
   notifications. Every fact is data the phone already holds; no new persisted state:
   - name: `loadSelf()?.name` non-nil (vault); picture: `loadAvatar() != .none` (vault);
   - currency: a stored `money.default-currency` that core knows (preference). **DECIDED (controller, for the
     founder): unset does not count**, though it resolves to the region's currency: the step is one tap to confirm,
     and counting it unset would make it a step nobody ever does;
   - app lock: `AppLockRules.lockedAtLaunch(timeout, available)` (preference + the phone's lock): the default (two
     minutes) counts on a phone with a lock, so the endowed progress is real;
   - first group: `namedGroups()` non-empty (vault; read-only copies count, you were in a group);
   - username: the account's username known (`People.myUsername()` / `UsernameState`), signed in only;
   - notifications: iOS authorisation `.authorized`, `.provisional` or `.ephemeral`; Android
     `areNotificationsEnabled() && Push.canPost(context)`; signed in only.
   The next step is the first undone in that order. While C2's `UsernameCard` shows (signed in, no username), the next
   card skips the username step (the card already asks).
