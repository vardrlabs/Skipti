SKIPTI privacy policy

Version 2, 20 October 2026. SKIPTI is made by Vardr Labs LLC.

The short version

SKIPTI has no ads, no trackers and no analytics, and we do not sell or share your data. Without an account, your ledger never leaves your phone. With an account, it is kept in SKIPTI's cloud on Google Firebase so your phones and your groups can share it. That cloud is protected by access rules, not end-to-end encryption.

Without an account

Your expenses, groups and settings are kept only on your phone, in an encrypted database. Nothing is sent anywhere. They leave the phone only if you export them.

With an account

You sign in with Apple or Google; they tell us an account id and the email address of that account (Apple may give a private relay address). We then keep, in SKIPTI's cloud:

  • Your ledger: the expenses, settlements and groups you record, with the names you give people and a group's picture if you add one.
  • Your profile: the name you show others, a username if you choose one, and a picture if you add one, shown small to your groups.
  • Your phones: each phone's name, type, app version, when it was last seen, and an address for notifications.
  • Your app settings: theme, light or dark, text size, Less motion, haptics, app icon and default currency, so your other phones match.
  • If you can be found (on unless you switch it off): a fingerprint (SHA-256) of your sign-in email, so people who know your email can find you.

Only you can read your own ledger and profile; only a group's members can read that group. The data is encrypted in transit and at rest, but not end to end: Google, as the host, and Vardr Labs, as the operator, could technically access it. It is stored in the United States (Google's nam5 region).

Contacts

Only if you tap "Find friends in contacts", SKIPTI reads your contacts' email addresses on your phone, turns each into a fingerprint there, and sends only the fingerprints to look for people on SKIPTI. The cloud keeps none of them, and no names, numbers or other contact details leave your phone.

Notifications

With notifications on, a message says who added, changed or deleted which expense, with the amount. Apple or Google delivers it to your phone.

Who else is involved

  • Google, which hosts SKIPTI's cloud (Firebase) and, like any web host, logs requests to it, including IP addresses, for 30 days.
  • Apple or Google, whichever you sign in with, and the one that delivers your notifications.

No one else. No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting company receives anything.

Your choices

  • Export everything, in an open format, from Settings, at any time.
  • Sync can be switched off in Settings; your phone then stops sending.
  • Being found can be switched off in Settings.
  • Your phones: see them and sign any of them out in Settings.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings, Delete account. Your account is deleted 7 days later unless you sign in and tap Keep my account. Deleted: your profile, username, phones, personal records, invitations and sign-in account; groups where you were the only member. What you added to a group with other members stays for them. The ledger on your phone stays until you erase it (Settings, Erase this phone).

Without the app: email skipti@vardrlabs.com from your sign-in address, or tell us which account it is, and we will delete it the same way.

Children

SKIPTI is not directed at children under 13.

Changes and contact

When this policy changes, its version and date change on this page. Questions: skipti@vardrlabs.com.
